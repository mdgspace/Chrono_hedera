About
Arc is Circle’s purpose-built, EVM-compatible L1 blockchain and the Economic OS for the internet. Arc Studio is the fastest way to build and deploy on it. Describe the onchain app you want, and Arc Studio works out what it needs in one place, including writing and testing any smart contracts, then builds the app around them. It has access to Arc and Circle products (USDC, EURC, Gateway, CCTP, Wallets) and other onchain skills, and you can bring your own skills into Arc Studio too. Every Arc prize requires using Arc Studio as the starting point. Please state which bounty you’re submitting for in your submission.
